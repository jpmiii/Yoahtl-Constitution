# **Article V. The Legislature** {#article-v.-the-legislature}

1. ## **Ololtic Assembly** {#ololtic-assembly}

   **Ololtic Assembly** is made up of citizens of Yoahtl operate as the abstract legislative body and can call for an assembly to address concerns, current-events, or organize events.  
1. All citizens are afforded one voluntary vote when voting on proposals, admitting cabinet members and affirming new citizens to the Ololtic.  
2. Ololtic Proposals, amendments to the charter, and other enactments will abide by the following procedure:   
   1. Citizens may submit proposals at any time regarding any legal, constitutional, or other enactment based issue.  
   2. After a period of discussion of a minimum of 24 hours during which the specifics of the law are discussed and revisions are suggested, a different member of the Ololtic can "second" the proposal. It enters a 48-hour period where it is voted on, during which discussion may continue.  
   3. If the proposer wishes to include revisions from additional discussions, they may update their proposal, at which point both the voting period and votes reset.  
   4. A proposal passes when it receives more ayes than nays and at least one-third (⅓) of all the members of the Ololtic has voted aye.  
   5. If there is a unanimous decision among the Ololtic then the proposal automatically passes, skipping the remaining voting period.  
   6. Votes can be changed whenever during the voting period.

2. ## **National Conference** {#national-conference}

1. The Council is able to call upon a national conference in order to discuss important matters with all pertaining parties involved so long as the time availability is reasonable.
