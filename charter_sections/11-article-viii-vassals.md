# **Article VIII. Vassals** {#article-viii.-vassals}

1. ## **Procedure** {#procedure}

   Any nation or groups of people who want to become a **vassal** of Yoahtl may be allowed to under the approval of the government. The government and the group of people will agree upon the rules which define the vassal and its connection to Yoahtl, what freedoms the vassal has and other issues which would be brought up in discussion between the government and the future vassal.
