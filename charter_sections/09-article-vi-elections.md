# **Article VI. Elections** {#article-vi.-elections}

1. ## **Council Elections** {#council-elections}

   **Council Elections** will happen every two months, where the Ololtic shall run for and vote to fill councillor seats.  
   1. The council shall be composed of 4 members minimum, with additional seats added for every 6 Ololtic members who have voted beyond 16\.  
   2. Only active members of the Ololtic when the election began are eligible to run for Council or vote in Council elections.  
      1. The Ololtic member must primarily live in Yoahtl to run for council.  
   3. A councillor cannot be part of another government while sitting on the council.  
   4. Each Olotic member is permitted 3 votes per voting cycle. Each member of the Ololtic may vote for as many councillors as the minimum number of council seats corresponding to Section VI.1.A.  
   5. Votes submitted cannot be changed unless in an event a candidate that they voted for has dropped out from the election and said candidate was mentioned in their ballot. After a member of the Ololtic has sent in their vote, it cannot be changed unless a candidate they voted for has dropped out.  
   6. Council elections will start on the First of every odd numbered month. The election period will last 7 days  
      1. Candidates may announce their candidacy between the first and third days of the election period. Voting shall occur between the fourth and seventh days of the election period.  
      2. Debates may occur during the entire election period.  
   7. The final count can't be revealed until the end of the voting period.  
   8. One can vote privately or publicly.   
      1. To vote privately the citizen must send their vote to the Alcuahtl.  
      2. To vote publicly the citizen must send their vote into the voting thread/channel.   
   9. In the event the Alcuahtl is unable to count the votes, the High Justice will count the votes. This can be considered vice versa in case of emergencies.  
   10. Directly or indirectly encouraging voters with gifts or monetary funds to vote in a certain way is strictly forbidden. Paying for political advertisements is seen as a form of expression.

2. ## **Interim Elections** {#interim-elections}

   **Interim elections** are elections held outside the normal two-month cycles to fill a vacant council seat which can accrue as defined in section III.1.C.  
1. An interim election should be held within one week of a resignation or impeachment to choose an official replacement, who will serve until the end of the current Council tenure.  
2. If the resignation or impeachment is within two weeks from the end of the Council tenure, the council seat stays empty until the next election.

3. ## **Recall Elections** {#recall-elections}

   **Recall elections** are allowed to be advanced by petition of the Ololtic. If the petition receives more than half of the submitted votes from the previous election, and the High Justice finds no evidence of collusion or bad faith, and that the given reason is valid, then an interim election will be held to replace the removed Councillor(s).  
   1. Such petitions ought not to be started by other government officials.
