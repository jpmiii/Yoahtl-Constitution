# **Preamble** {#preamble}

As time goes on, it becomes necessary for a nation to establish for itself a body of laws to ensure domestic order and the preservation of the values that define it. So too is it for Yoahtl, which has for almost a decade now existed without a charter to define its government and protect its core \- that all people, regardless of origin, beliefs, politics, or characteristics, are welcome and accepted here, so long as they work for the betterment of the community as a whole.

# **Article I. The Bill of Rights** {#article-i.-the-bill-of-rights}

The Bill of Rights applies to all Yoahtlans. No law or edict may be created that infringes upon these inalienable rights. These rights may be suspended as part of punishment for a crime they have been duly convicted of.

1. All Yoahtlans have the right to freedom of expression.  
2. All Yoahtlans have the right to self-defense against an unlawful aggressor.  
3. All Yoahtlans have the right to freedom of belief and religion.  
4. All Yoahtlans have the right to a timely and just trial.  
5. All Yoahtlans have the right to privacy.  
6. All Yoahtlans have the right to peaceful assembly and protest.  
7. All Yoahtlans have the right to be free from discrimination and harassment.

# **Article II. Citizenry** {#article-ii.-citizenry}

1. ## **The Residency** {#the-residency}

   **Residents** are identified as an individual who lives in Yohatl or otherwise participates in the nation and is not a member of the Ololtic. They must be approved by the government beforehand in order to operate within Yoahtl territory. Once approved, Residency must be given to anyone who lives legally in Yoahtl by the government.  
   1. Residents are not citizens of Yoahtl and only citizens of the settlement they reside in.  
   2. Residents can gain their status via opening a ticket in the Yoahtl discord and being approved.  
   3. Residents can lose their status if the government finds there is probable cause and reasonable suspicion that would warrant a removal.  
   4. Residents can submit a request to become a member of the Ololtic at any time after being active in Yoahtl and fulfilling the requirements to be considered applicable.  
   5. Listed activities for Residents:  
      1. Residents can:  
         1. Apply for plot within a city or town  
         2. Apply for a homestead request  
         3. Build and operate a Business with approval from the government  
         4. Participate in civil and general discussion  
         5. Participate in Community Events if applicable.  
         6. Take up work on Public Projects if applicable.  
      2. Residents cannot:  
         1. Vote on any proposals, confirming nominees to public office, affirming new citizens to the Ololtic or anything else.  
         2. Run for government positions of any kind.

2. ## **The Ololtic** {#the-ololtic}

   **Ololtic** (i.e. citizens or citizenship) denotes the abstract legislative body that consists of all voting-eligible members of Yoahtl. It shall comprise of members voted in by other members of the Ololtic as designated by this Charter. Ololtic are individuals that are identified as Yoahtlan citizens who have met the proper requirements to be recognized as such.  
1. New members of the Ololtic shall be added as follows:  
   1. A formal request is submitted.  
      2. A government member then begins the voting process during which current members of the ololtic have 48 hours to vote on the request.  
      3. Once the 48 hour period has passed, if the request has received majority (50%+1) ayes from the ololtic then it passes.

3. ## **Suspension** {#suspension}

1. Citizenship may be suspended on grounds of inactivity.  
   1. If a person with suspended citizenship has become active again, and has shown the intent to continue to be active, then they may request to a councillor that their citizenship is unsuspended.  
2. Any previous citizen of Yoahtl not voted into the Ololtic as established by this charter shall regain citizenship rights by Ololtic vote.   
3. Citizenship can be removed if there is probable cause via court decision or through a Ololtic proposal.

   

# **Article III. The Executive** {#article-iii.-the-executive}

1. ## **The Council** {#the-council}

   The **Council** is the directorial body of Yoahtl, serving collectively as the executive body for both national and foreign affairs.  
1. The Council will be responsible for, but not limited to, the construction and maintenance of public factories, roads, and other public property; for creating and enforcing laws, taxes, and tariffs; for organizing a monthly census of active citizens; for approving or rejecting binding treaties with foreign powers; and for carrying out other duties necessary for maintaining a strong and positive community within the nation.   
   1. All members of the Council are to be considered co-equals, regardless of designated sub-role within the operation of the government.   
   2. Councillors will be elected to a term of 2 months as outlined in section VI.1.  
   3. The Council is responsible for electing the Alcuahtl to be "first among equals" from among their fellow councillors.   
   4. The Council shall meet regularly to jointly determine the direction of the nation and plans for its future development and growth.  
2. The Council, with the approval of the Alcuahtl or General as the head of the armed forces, is responsible for voting on whether to declare war on a foreign power. A two-thirds (⅔) majority vote and the approval of the Head of the Armed forces is required to declare war.  
   1. If an act of war is committed against Yoahtl, it is the responsibility of the government to take action to disperse the threat. Defensive actions do not require a declaration of war by the empire of Yoahtl.  
3. Councillors may resign or be removed so long there is probable cause.  
   1. Prior notice must be given 24-48 hours if the Councilor is unable to fulfill their responsibilities within Council Chambers.  
   2. In the event that a Councilor resigns, Councillors may have an interim election if it is deemed necessary.  
   3. In the event that a Councilor is impeached, the High Justice and Alcuahtl must have probable cause to do so.

2. ## **Executive decisions** {#executive-decisions}

   **Executive decisions** also known as council acts are to be determined via majority (50%+1) vote among all members of the council.   
1. These include but are not limited to:  
   1. Council Acts are laws voted upon, and passed without the Ololtic.  
   2. Treaties or agreements with nations, polities, and people.  
   3. Military operations.  
   4. Territory changes.  
2. Routine council duties (eg. Alcuahtl confirmations, other military duties, trade deals, derelictions, censuses, and other non-Act decisions) do not count as Executive Decisions, even if they involve a council vote.  
3. Should a vote be tied, the High Justice will be called in to break the stalemate.  
4. Executive Decisions cannot override laws passed by the Ololtic or amend the Charter.

3. ## **The Alcuahtl** {#the-alcuahtl}

   **The Alcuahtl** should facilitate council business, announce and conduct meetings, act as head of government to coordinate the population, and be the lead diplomat of Yoahtl.  
1. There are no term limits to the office of Alcuahtl, consecutive or otherwise.  
2. The Alcuahtl should act as the primary representative of the Yoahtl populace in matters of foreign diplomacy, and is in charge of speaking with the authority of the government to other national leaders.  
3. The Alcuahtl will be the head of the Yoahtlan army, and supreme commander of the Yoahtlan armed forces.  
4. When a Council Election period is over, the Council will nominate another councilor or themselves to be Alcuahtl. After everyone has nominated someone or themselves, the Council votes on who they want to be the Alcuahtl out of the nominees.  
   1. The vote requires a majority (50%+1) in the favour for one of the nominees to be chosen.  
   2. The Alcuahtl must be chosen within a reasonable timespan after the Election period.  
   3. Should the vote be tied, the High Justice will be called in to break the stalemate.  
5. The Alcuahtl has the power to exile a non-ololtic player from Yoahtl and ban them from entering Yoahtlan territory.  
   1. An Alcuahtl ban or sentence of exile may be overturned by a two-thirds (⅔) majority vote of the Council.  
6. Should the Alcuahtl no longer represent the interests of Yoahtl or have become too inactive, they may be removed from office by way of unanimous (minus the Alcuahtl themself) Council vote or by Court ruling.

4. ## **The Cabinet** {#the-cabinet}

1. Councillors can assign dedicated roles to perform within the government.  
   1. These roles can be assigned to councillors and Ololtics, who are trusted to perform the role granted to them.  
      1. The Council may appoint an Ololtic to perform a cabinet role with approval from the Ololtic.  
   2. A member of the may be only removed with probable cause proof of wrong doing, or in the case of a non-Councillor member, can be with a two-thirds (⅔) majority from the Council.  
   3. The member may delegate a duty after informing the council and within reason.  
   4. If the roles listed below are not filled (III.4.B.), the duty falls upon the entire collective council if not specified.  
   5. A person can have multiple cabinet roles at once.   
2. The Roles that can be assigned:  
   1. **The Deputy Alcuahtl** may be appointed by the Alcuahtl or by Council if the Alcuahtl is absent for an extended period of time (including situations of real life vacation or other real life reasons), and will take over the duties of the Alcuahtl until the Alcuahtl is available to perform their duties again. The Deputy Alcuahtl role is not required to be filled when a Council forms.   
      1. Deputy Alcuahtl can only be filled by councillors.   
      2. **The General** is the leading Military member of the Cabinet and will dedicate their time to military projects and defense. If the General position is not filled, the Alcuahtl takes up general duties.  
         1. The General can only be filled by the government or members in The Military.  
      3. **The Auditor-General** is responsible for monitoring the supply of government resources (primarily liquid assets and XP). They must account for all transactions within reason, and follow-up and report on any discrepancies. The Auditor-General must ideally not be a Citizen heavily involved in production or expenditure (I.e XP production or defense). The Auditor-General must also keep track of all assets that the government controls. The monitoring of financial assets cannot be delegated.  
         1. The Auditor General can only be filled by councillors.  
      4. **The Cuitcahtl** must be involved with the government-run exchanges and is required to maintain the government exchanges. The Exchanger-General is incharge of leading the development of Farms. The Exchanger-General may delegate with Council approval.   
         1. The Cuitcahtl can be filled by Ololtic and Council.  
      5. **The Derelictor-General** is responsible for dereliction duty, maintenance and creation of buildings and civilian infrastructure. The Derelictor-General has discretion over the need to derelict, but cannot abuse the power.  
         1. The Derelictor-General can be filled by Ololtic and Council.  
      6. **The Diplomat** is a role given out by the Alcuahtl to a Councillor or Ololtic to do diplomacy with other polities and groups for Yoahtl.  
         1. Diplomacy does not include tickets with individual people/persons  
         2. The Diplomat can be filled by Ololtic and Council.  
      7. **The High Tlatoani** is the main recruiter of newcomers to Yoahtl, and the organizer of the Tlatoani. The High Tlatoani should focus on creation of Events and organizing newcomers to perform tasks for Yoahtl. High Tlatoani will also gather jobs from the Tlatoani and Council to post for Residents to view.  
         1. If this role isn't filled it also falls upon the Ololtic and Residents, not just the Council.   
         2. The High Tlatoani can be filled by Ololtic and Council.

# **Article IV. The Judiciary** {#article-iv.-the-judiciary}

Yoahtl’s laws are supreme in its territory. All pearls taken on Yoahtlan land must be tried by Yoahtlan courts, under Yoahtlan law.

1. ## **The High Justice** {#the-high-justice}

1. **The High Justice** will be appointed by the Ololtic and serve indefinitely.  
2. The High Justice will not preside over trials directly, but will nominate a judge.  
   1. If the court case is against or made by the government the Judge must be the High Justice.  
3. The judge must be a member of the Council who does not have a personal interest in the trial’s outcome.  
4. The High Justice is forced to resign if the Ololtic votes and the ayes represent two-thirds (⅔) of the vote.  
5. The High Justice cannot have any other government positions.  
   1. In the case of a government member being nominated and passed as the new High justice, they would need to resign from their other government roles.   
6. The High Justice has the exclusive right to declare mistrials.

2. ## **Replacement of The High Justice** {#replacement-of-the-high-justice}

   1. In the case of a High Justice resigning or being forced to resign as defined in section IV.1.D. this would be the procedure to choose another High Justice:  
   1. There will be a nomination period which starts after the resignation/impeachment of the High Justice where the Ololtic nominates another Ololtic member or themselves to be High Justice.   
   2. The nomination period lasts two days.   
   3. If someone who was nominated doesn't want to be High Justice, their nomination won't be continued to the voting period.  
   4. After the nomination period, there will be a voting period where the nominees will be voted upon. where each Ololtic member gets one vote.   
      1. If the vote doesn't lead to a nominee having more then half (50%+1) of the vote then there will be runoff votes until there's one nominee with over half (50%+1) of the vote.   
   5. In the case of a tie between two nominees the Alcuahtl will be called in to break the tie.

# **Article V. The Legislature** {#article-v.-the-legislature}

1. ## **Ololtic Assembly** {#ololtic-assembly}

   **Ololtic Assembly** is made up of citizens of Yoahtl operate as the abstract legislative body and can call for an assembly to address concerns, current-events, or organize events.  
1. All citizens are afforded one voluntary vote when voting on proposals, admitting cabinet members and affirming new citizens to the Ololtic.  
2. Ololtic Proposals, amendments to the charter, and other enactments will abide by the following procedure:   
   1. Citizens may submit proposals at any time regarding any legal, constitutional, or other enactment based issue.  
   2. After a period of discussion of a minimum of 24 hours during which the specifics of the law are discussed and revisions are suggested, a different member of the Ololtic can "second" the proposal. It enters a 48-hour period where it is voted on, during which discussion may continue.  
   3. If the proposer wishes to include revisions from additional discussions, they may update their proposal, at which point both the voting period and votes reset.  
   4. A proposal passes when it receives more ayes than nays and at least one-third (⅓) of all the members of the Ololtic has voted aye.  
   5. If there is a unanimous decision among the Ololtic then the proposal automatically passes, skipping the remaining voting period.  
   6. Votes can be changed whenever during the voting period.

2. ## **National Conference** {#national-conference}

1. The Council is able to call upon a national conference in order to discuss important matters with all pertaining parties involved so long as the time availability is reasonable. 

# **Article VI. Elections** {#article-vi.-elections}

1. ## **Council Elections** {#council-elections}

   **Council Elections** will happen every two months, where the Ololtic shall run for and vote to fill councillor seats.  
   1. The council shall be composed of 4 members minimum, with additional seats added for every 6 Ololtic members who have voted beyond 16\.  
   2. Only active members of the Ololtic when the election began are eligible to run for Council or vote in Council elections.  
      1. The Ololtic member must primarily live in Yoahtl to run for council.  
   3. A councillor cannot be part of another government while sitting on the council.  
   4. Each Olotic member is permitted 3 votes per voting cycle. Each member of the Ololtic may vote for as many councillors as the minimum number of council seats corresponding to Section VI.1.A.  
   5. Votes submitted cannot be changed unless in an event a candidate that they voted for has dropped out from the election and said candidate was mentioned in their ballot. After a member of the Ololtic has sent in their vote, it cannot be changed unless a candidate they voted for has dropped out.  
   6. Council elections will start on the First of every odd numbered month. The election period will last 7 days  
      1. Candidates may announce their candidacy between the first and third days of the election period. Voting shall occur between the fourth and seventh days of the election period.  
      2. Debates may occur during the entire election period.  
   7. The final count can't be revealed until the end of the voting period.  
   8. One can vote privately or publicly.   
      1. To vote privately the citizen must send their vote to the Alcuahtl.  
      2. To vote publicly the citizen must send their vote into the voting thread/channel.   
   9. In the event the Alcuahtl is unable to count the votes, the High Justice will count the votes. This can be considered vice versa in case of emergencies.  
   10. Directly or indirectly encouraging voters with gifts or monetary funds to vote in a certain way is strictly forbidden. Paying for political advertisements is seen as a form of expression.

2. ## **Interim Elections** {#interim-elections}

   **Interim elections** are elections held outside the normal two-month cycles to fill a vacant council seat which can accrue as defined in section III.1.C.  
1. An interim election should be held within one week of a resignation or impeachment to choose an official replacement, who will serve until the end of the current Council tenure.  
2. If the resignation or impeachment is within two weeks from the end of the Council tenure, the council seat stays empty until the next election.

3. ## **Recall Elections** {#recall-elections}

   **Recall elections** are allowed to be advanced by petition of the Ololtic. If the petition receives more than half of the submitted votes from the previous election, and the High Justice finds no evidence of collusion or bad faith, and that the given reason is valid, then an interim election will be held to replace the removed Councillor(s).  
   1. Such petitions ought not to be started by other government officials.

# **Article VII. Land Use** {#article-vii.-land-use}

Territory of the State of Yoahtl consists of all lands claimed by the Government of Yoahtl otherwise known as Yoahtl; the Empire of the Rising Dawn.

1. ## **Personal** {#personal}

   1. **Plots** are areas designated for use by an individual or group, or for a specific purpose, by the local or national government. It is required to submit a plot request and needs approval from:  
      1. The Council in the capital city.  
      2. The local government of the town the individual is requesting the plot in.  
   2. Any plot which has been designated for use but stays undeveloped, unfinished, or unused for a sufficient period of time may be returned to the government and derelicted.   
   3. **Homesteads** are personal plots of land in Yoahtlan territory outside of Town boundaries requested by an individual which requires Council approval prior to modifications. The owner of the homestead has the right to modify or improve the property within boundaries demarcated, discussed, and approved by the Council. It is suggested to have a general idea of what you have in mind before submitting a proposal and discuss the details properly with Council.  
   4. Military infrastructure not belonging to the government may not be built on homesteads or plots without approval from the Council, with the exception of reasonably sized personal obsidian boxes.  
   5. Plot and homestead requests must be approved by a majority (50%+1) of the Council.

2. ## **Designated** {#designated}

   1. **Towns** are areas designated by a set boundary approved by Council for individuals led by a singular or group of Tlatoani. The Tlatoani are tasked with maintaining and developing the land designated to the town. If no active residents remain in the town, Council may appoint a Tlatoani to run the town or a temporary guardian to watch over the town to prevent any grief from occurring.  
   2. **Facilities** are areas that are built to serve a particular purpose in mind. It will often be built along the demand amongst the population of Yoahtl or nation’s interest.   
   3. **Public Farms** are built to scale in demand with the production values in mind. These are normally publicly accessible to Yoahtl citizens to freely use so long they treat the farms with care.

3. ## **Protected** {#protected}

   1. **Historical** areas are places that hold significant value to the community. It is generally marked as such by the government.  
   2. **Recreation** areas are community made areas that are meant for everyone to enjoy. This often includes parks, trails, or other builds that allow people to hang around.

4. ## **Restricted** {#restricted}

   1. Restricted areas may include military infrastructure and Exclusion Zones.  
   2. Any area can be designated as Restricted if approved by the government and military. 

# **Article VIII. Vassals** {#article-viii.-vassals}

1. ## **Procedure** {#procedure}

   Any nation or groups of people who want to become a **vassal** of Yoahtl may be allowed to under the approval of the government. The government and the group of people will agree upon the rules which define the vassal and its connection to Yoahtl, what freedoms the vassal has and other issues which would be brought up in discussion between the government and the future vassal.

# **Article IX. Amendments** {#article-ix.-amendments}

1. ##  **Charter Changes** {#charter-changes}

   1. Amendments to this Charter may be made via the standard Ololtic proposal process, but instead require the Ayes to represent two-thirds (⅔) of the Ololtic.  
   2. If any Grammatical errors are discovered they may be corrected without a proper proposal with the approval of the High Justice.  
      1. All errors and changes are to be recorded with the details of who, what, when, and why.

2. ##  **Charter Suspension**  {#charter-suspension}

   1. This Charter may be suspended upon a vote from the Ololtic which affirmative votes constitute no less than four-fifths (⅘) of its membership.  
   2. In the event that a majority of the government is inactive and incapable of replacement, the remaining members of Yoahtl may suspend this Charter upon a vote in which the Ayes constitute no less than two-third (⅔) of participating members. Upon such suspension, the remaining members of the Ololtic shall establish a temporary government to administer Yoahtl until a new Charter has been approved and enacted.
