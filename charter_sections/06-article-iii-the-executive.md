# **Article III. The Executive** {#article-iii.-the-executive}

1. ## **The Council** {#the-council}

   The **Council** is the directorial body of Yoahtl, serving collectively as the executive body for both national and foreign affairs.  
1. The Council will be responsible for, but not limited to, the construction and maintenance of public factories, roads, and other public property; for creating and enforcing laws, taxes, and tariffs; for organizing a monthly census of active citizens; for approving or rejecting binding treaties with foreign powers; and for carrying out other duties necessary for maintaining a strong and positive community within the nation.   
   1. All members of the Council are to be considered co-equals, regardless of designated sub-role within the operation of the government.   
   2. Councillors will be elected to a term of 2 months as outlined in section VI.1.  
   3. The Council is responsible for electing the Alcuahtl to be "first among equals" from among their fellow councillors.   
   4. The Council shall meet regularly to jointly determine the direction of the nation and plans for its future development and growth.  
2. The Council, with the approval of the Alcuahtl or General as the head of the armed forces, is responsible for voting on whether to declare war on a foreign power. A two-thirds (⅔) majority vote and the approval of the Head of the Armed forces is required to declare war.  
   1. If an act of war is committed against Yoahtl, it is the responsibility of the government to take action to disperse the threat. Defensive actions do not require a declaration of war by the empire of Yoahtl.  
3. Councillors may resign or be removed so long there is probable cause.  
   1. Prior notice must be given 24-48 hours if the Councilor is unable to fulfill their responsibilities within Council Chambers.  
   2. In the event that a Councilor resigns, Councillors may have an interim election if it is deemed necessary.  
   3. In the event that a Councilor is impeached, the High Justice and Alcuahtl must have probable cause to do so.

2. ## **Executive decisions** {#executive-decisions}

   **Executive decisions** also known as council acts are to be determined via majority (50%+1) vote among all members of the council.   
1. These include but are not limited to:  
   1. Council Acts are laws voted upon, and passed without the Ololtic.  
   2. Treaties or agreements with nations, polities, and people.  
   3. Military operations.  
   4. Territory changes.  
2. Routine council duties (eg. Alcuahtl confirmations, other military duties, trade deals, derelictions, censuses, and other non-Act decisions) do not count as Executive Decisions, even if they involve a council vote.  
3. Should a vote be tied, the High Justice will be called in to break the stalemate.  
4. Executive Decisions cannot override laws passed by the Ololtic or amend the Charter.

3. ## **The Alcuahtl** {#the-alcuahtl}

   **The Alcuahtl** should facilitate council business, announce and conduct meetings, act as head of government to coordinate the population, and be the lead diplomat of Yoahtl.  
1. There are no term limits to the office of Alcuahtl, consecutive or otherwise.  
2. The Alcuahtl should act as the primary representative of the Yoahtl populace in matters of foreign diplomacy, and is in charge of speaking with the authority of the government to other national leaders.  
3. The Alcuahtl will be the head of the Yoahtlan army, and supreme commander of the Yoahtlan armed forces.  
4. When a Council Election period is over, the Council will nominate another councilor or themselves to be Alcuahtl. After everyone has nominated someone or themselves, the Council votes on who they want to be the Alcuahtl out of the nominees.  
   1. The vote requires a majority (50%+1) in the favour for one of the nominees to be chosen.  
   2. The Alcuahtl must be chosen within a reasonable timespan after the Election period.  
   3. Should the vote be tied, the High Justice will be called in to break the stalemate.  
5. The Alcuahtl has the power to exile a non-ololtic player from Yoahtl and ban them from entering Yoahtlan territory.  
   1. An Alcuahtl ban or sentence of exile may be overturned by a two-thirds (⅔) majority vote of the Council.  
6. Should the Alcuahtl no longer represent the interests of Yoahtl or have become too inactive, they may be removed from office by way of unanimous (minus the Alcuahtl themself) Council vote or by Court ruling.

4. ## **The Cabinet** {#the-cabinet}

1. Councillors can assign dedicated roles to perform within the government.  
   1. These roles can be assigned to councillors and Ololtics, who are trusted to perform the role granted to them.  
      1. The Council may appoint an Ololtic to perform a cabinet role with approval from the Ololtic.  
   2. A member of the may be only removed with probable cause proof of wrong doing, or in the case of a non-Councillor member, can be with a two-thirds (⅔) majority from the Council.  
   3. The member may delegate a duty after informing the council and within reason.  
   4. If the roles listed below are not filled (III.4.B.), the duty falls upon the entire collective council if not specified.  
   5. A person can have multiple cabinet roles at once.   
2. The Roles that can be assigned:  
   1. **The Deputy Alcuahtl** may be appointed by the Alcuahtl or by Council if the Alcuahtl is absent for an extended period of time (including situations of real life vacation or other real life reasons), and will take over the duties of the Alcuahtl until the Alcuahtl is available to perform their duties again. The Deputy Alcuahtl role is not required to be filled when a Council forms.   
      1. Deputy Alcuahtl can only be filled by councillors.   
      2. **The General** is the leading Military member of the Cabinet and will dedicate their time to military projects and defense. If the General position is not filled, the Alcuahtl takes up general duties.  
         1. The General can only be filled by the government or members in The Military.  
      3. **The Auditor-General** is responsible for monitoring the supply of government resources (primarily liquid assets and XP). They must account for all transactions within reason, and follow-up and report on any discrepancies. The Auditor-General must ideally not be a Citizen heavily involved in production or expenditure (I.e XP production or defense). The Auditor-General must also keep track of all assets that the government controls. The monitoring of financial assets cannot be delegated.  
         1. The Auditor General can only be filled by councillors.  
      4. **The Cuitcahtl** must be involved with the government-run exchanges and is required to maintain the government exchanges. The Exchanger-General is incharge of leading the development of Farms. The Exchanger-General may delegate with Council approval.   
         1. The Cuitcahtl can be filled by Ololtic and Council.  
      5. **The Derelictor-General** is responsible for dereliction duty, maintenance and creation of buildings and civilian infrastructure. The Derelictor-General has discretion over the need to derelict, but cannot abuse the power.  
         1. The Derelictor-General can be filled by Ololtic and Council.  
      6. **The Diplomat** is a role given out by the Alcuahtl to a Councillor or Ololtic to do diplomacy with other polities and groups for Yoahtl.  
         1. Diplomacy does not include tickets with individual people/persons  
         2. The Diplomat can be filled by Ololtic and Council.  
      7. **The High Tlatoani** is the main recruiter of newcomers to Yoahtl, and the organizer of the Tlatoani. The High Tlatoani should focus on creation of Events and organizing newcomers to perform tasks for Yoahtl. High Tlatoani will also gather jobs from the Tlatoani and Council to post for Residents to view.  
         1. If this role isn't filled it also falls upon the Ololtic and Residents, not just the Council.   
         2. The High Tlatoani can be filled by Ololtic and Council.
