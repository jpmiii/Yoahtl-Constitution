# **Article VII. Land Use** {#article-vii.-land-use}

Territory of the State of Yoahtl consists of all lands claimed by the Government of Yoahtl otherwise known as Yoahtl; the Empire of the Rising Dawn.

1. ## **Personal** {#personal}

   1. **Plots** are areas designated for use by an individual or group, or for a specific purpose, by the local or national government. It is required to submit a plot request and needs approval from:  
      1. The Council in the capital city.  
      2. The local government of the town the individual is requesting the plot in.  
   2. Any plot which has been designated for use but stays undeveloped, unfinished, or unused for a sufficient period of time may be returned to the government and derelicted.   
   3. **Homesteads** are personal plots of land in Yoahtlan territory outside of Town boundaries requested by an individual which requires Council approval prior to modifications. The owner of the homestead has the right to modify or improve the property within boundaries demarcated, discussed, and approved by the Council. It is suggested to have a general idea of what you have in mind before submitting a proposal and discuss the details properly with Council.  
   4. Military infrastructure not belonging to the government may not be built on homesteads or plots without approval from the Council, with the exception of reasonably sized personal obsidian boxes.  
   5. Plot and homestead requests must be approved by a majority (50%+1) of the Council.

2. ## **Designated** {#designated}

   1. **Towns** are areas designated by a set boundary approved by Council for individuals led by a singular or group of Tlatoani. The Tlatoani are tasked with maintaining and developing the land designated to the town. If no active residents remain in the town, Council may appoint a Tlatoani to run the town or a temporary guardian to watch over the town to prevent any grief from occurring.  
   2. **Facilities** are areas that are built to serve a particular purpose in mind. It will often be built along the demand amongst the population of Yoahtl or nation’s interest.   
   3. **Public Farms** are built to scale in demand with the production values in mind. These are normally publicly accessible to Yoahtl citizens to freely use so long they treat the farms with care.

3. ## **Protected** {#protected}

   1. **Historical** areas are places that hold significant value to the community. It is generally marked as such by the government.  
   2. **Recreation** areas are community made areas that are meant for everyone to enjoy. This often includes parks, trails, or other builds that allow people to hang around.

4. ## **Restricted** {#restricted}

   1. Restricted areas may include military infrastructure and Exclusion Zones.  
   2. Any area can be designated as Restricted if approved by the government and military.
