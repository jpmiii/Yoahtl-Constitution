# **Article II. Citizenry** {#article-ii.-citizenry}

1. ## **The Residency** {#the-residency}

   **Residents** are identified as an individual who lives in Yohatl or otherwise participates in the nation and is not a member of the Ololtic. They must be approved by the government beforehand in order to operate within Yoahtl territory. Once approved, Residency must be given to anyone who lives legally in Yoahtl by the government.  
   1. Residents are not citizens of Yoahtl and only citizens of the settlement they reside in.  
   2. Residents can gain their status via opening a ticket in the Yoahtl discord and being approved.  
   3. Residents can lose their status if the government finds there is probable cause and reasonable suspicion that would warrant a removal.  
   4. Residents can submit a request to become a member of the Ololtic at any time after being active in Yoahtl and fulfilling the requirements to be considered applicable.  
   5. Listed activities for Residents:  
      1. Residents can:  
         1. Apply for plot within a city or town  
         2. Apply for a homestead request  
         3. Build and operate a Business with approval from the government  
         4. Participate in civil and general discussion  
         5. Participate in Community Events if applicable.  
         6. Take up work on Public Projects if applicable.  
      2. Residents cannot:  
         1. Vote on any proposals, confirming nominees to public office, affirming new citizens to the Ololtic or anything else.  
         2. Run for government positions of any kind.

2. ## **The Ololtic** {#the-ololtic}

   **Ololtic** (i.e. citizens or citizenship) denotes the abstract legislative body that consists of all voting-eligible members of Yoahtl. It shall comprise of members voted in by other members of the Ololtic as designated by this Charter. Ololtic are individuals that are identified as Yoahtlan citizens who have met the proper requirements to be recognized as such.  
1. New members of the Ololtic shall be added as follows:  
   1. A formal request is submitted.  
      2. A government member then begins the voting process during which current members of the ololtic have 48 hours to vote on the request.  
      3. Once the 48 hour period has passed, if the request has received majority (50%+1) ayes from the ololtic then it passes.

3. ## **Suspension** {#suspension}

1. Citizenship may be suspended on grounds of inactivity.  
   1. If a person with suspended citizenship has become active again, and has shown the intent to continue to be active, then they may request to a councillor that their citizenship is unsuspended.  
2. Any previous citizen of Yoahtl not voted into the Ololtic as established by this charter shall regain citizenship rights by Ololtic vote.   
3. Citizenship can be removed if there is probable cause via court decision or through a Ololtic proposal.
